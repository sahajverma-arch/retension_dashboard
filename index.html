What the numbers mean for leadership

!
Critical analyst caveat — read before acting on the trend. Renewals occur over time. The most recent cohorts (late-2025 → 2026) have not yet passed through their full renewal window, so their low renewal rates are partly right-censoring (incomplete observation), not only genuine deterioration. The declining trend is therefore a blend of (a) real weakening visible in mature cohorts and (b) young cohorts still in flight. Every forecast below is flagged accordingly. This distinction is observed evidence vs. interpretation — treat it as such.

Method, Assumptions & Confidence

Limitations — read before quoting a number
AI
  • Short series & structural break. ~24 monthly points with a clear regime shift; a straight line understates uncertainty.
  • Right-censoring. Recent cohorts are immature — fitting a trend through them biases the slope downward. The true forward rate is likely above the naive line.
  • No exogenous inputs. Pricing, seasonality drivers, campaigns, and cohort size are not in the dataset.
  • Percentages only. No customer counts, so intervals are on rates, not revenue or headcount.
  • We do not claim any prediction is “100% accurate”.
